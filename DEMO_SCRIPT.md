# DEMO SCRIPT & VIDEO TIMELINE: TouchGrass AI

## Target Duration: 2 Minutes (120–140 seconds)
**Core Message:** The screen is the shortest part of the experience. Local open-source AI dispatches your micro-expedition and verifies your outdoor observation with zero cloud telemetry.

---

### [00:00 – 00:15] Scene 1: The Problem
- **Visual:** Close-up of a developer staring at multiple IDE screens, slack messages popping up, looking fatigued.
- **Voiceover:** "We spend 8 to 12 hours a day trapped behind monitors. When we finally get a 15-minute break, doomscrolling pulls us right back into screens. Traditional trail apps make it worse: they demand you stare at turn-by-turn navigation the entire time."

---

### [00:15 – 00:40] Scene 2: The Solution & Minimum Screen Time
- **Visual:** Open TouchGrass AI interface. The screen timer starts at 00:01.
- **Action:** User taps "15 Min" and "Public Park", then clicks **"⚡ Dispatch Expedition & Leave Screen"**.
- **Voiceover:** "Meet TouchGrass AI. It is designed around one strict rule: the screen must be the shortest part of the experience. In under 5 seconds, our system synthesizes a sensory outdoor quest grounded in your available time and biome."

---

### [00:40 – 01:10] Scene 3: Pocket Mode & Disconnect Cue
- **Visual:** Quest reveals: *Canopy & Vein Recon*. Off-screen sensory tasks are displayed. User clicks **"📴 Enter Pocket Mode & Step Outside"**.
- **Audio:** Calming harmonic chime plays.
- **Visual:** The screen immediately transitions to an ultra-clean, minimalist ambient pulse: *"Put your phone in your pocket. Look up. Breathe. Touch grass."* The phone is slipped into a coat or pocket.
- **Voiceover:** "Once dispatched, the screen interaction is finished. Pocket mode activates with a gentle departure chime. The phone goes into your pocket, and physical nature takes over."

---

### [01:10 – 01:40] Scene 4: Real-World Action (Touching Grass)
- **Visual:** Cut to outdoor footage. Walking along a tree-lined park path. Touching rough tree bark. Looking up at the tree canopy. Kneeling down to observe an intricate fallen leaf on the grass.
- **Action:** Snapping exactly 1 photograph of the leaf.
- **Voiceover:** "For the next 15 minutes, there are no notifications, feeds, or screens. You observe leaf architecture, feel the breeze, and touch real earth. Exactly one photo is taken of your field observation."

---

### [01:40 – 02:10] Scene 5: Local Open AI Verification
- **Visual:** Back at the workstation or park bench. User uploads the leaf photo.
- **Visual:** Taps **"🧠 Run Local AI Biophilia Analysis"**.
- **Action:** In **12.8 milliseconds**, the MobileNetV4 ONNX model classifies the image: *Daisy / Foliage (Flora)*. Grass Score: *98/100*.
- **Demonstration:** User tries uploading an indoor picture of a laptop keyboard. The model instantly rejects it: *"Indoor Tech Detected (Computer Keyboard). No grass detected! Get away from your desk!"*
- **Voiceover:** "Our core intelligence is powered by MobileNetV4 running 100% locally on CPU via ONNX Runtime in just 12 milliseconds. It verifies genuine biophilia and rejects indoor screens with zero cloud API keys."

---

### [02:10 – 02:25] Scene 6: Why Open Innovation Matters
- **Visual:** System architecture graphic highlighting local ONNX inference, SQLite offline storage, and Apache 2.0 open-weight model.
- **Voiceover:** "Because the model is open-weight and local, it works anywhere—even off the grid in remote forests. Your private photos never leave your device, and there are zero recurring API costs. Open-source AI got us off the screen and into the real world."
